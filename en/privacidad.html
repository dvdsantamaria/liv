Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 May 2026

1. Who we are

LIV Migration Australia provides migration and education guidance for people considering study, life or migration options in Australia. This policy explains how we collect, use, protect and share personal information when you interact with our website, forms, consultations, communications or services.

2. Information we may collect

  • Contact details such as name, email, phone number, country of residence and city.
  • Information related to your migration or education enquiry.
  • Study history, work experience, family circumstances, migration history and goals.
  • Documents you share with us to assess or prepare a matter.
  • Administrative, billing or payment information where relevant.
  • Basic website data such as pages visited, browser, device and usage metrics.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, coordinate meetings, assess scenarios, prepare migration or education services, manage documentation, meet professional and legal obligations, improve our website and maintain relevant communications with you.

4. When we may share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share it only where needed to provide the service, where you authorise us to do so, or where required by law. This may include government bodies, education providers, management platforms, professional advisers, technology providers or regulatory authorities.

5. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, professional obligations, legal requirements or legitimate record-keeping needs.

6. Access, correction and questions

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you or ask for corrections if you believe it is incomplete, outdated or inaccurate. You can also contact us with questions about this policy or how we handle your information.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, email admin@liv-consulting.com.au.

This policy was prepared with reference to general Australian privacy principles, including the Australian Privacy Principles administered by the OAIC.
